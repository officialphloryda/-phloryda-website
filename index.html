ABOUT PHLORYDA

Derick Chibueze Madu, widely known by his stage name PHLORYDA, is a Nigerian musician from the Eastern region of Nigeria and based in Rivers State. He is a songwriter, rapper and singer known for blending native Igbo and English into his distinctive musical style.

A graduate of Civil Engineering from Michael Okpara University of Agriculture, Umudike (MOUAU), PHLORYDA continues to build his place in the Nigerian music scene through his sound, energetic performances and consistent creative work.

His debut project, Scars, Tears And Reality (S.T.A.R), explores ambition, survival, reality, motivation and everyday life.

He has opened stages at JERIQ STADIUM CONCERT 2024 in Onitsha and AGUERO BANKS CONCERT 2024 in Enugu, sharing stages with artists including Bella Shmurda, Ajebo Hustlers, BlaqBonez and Wizard Chan. He has also performed at OY TO THE WORLD CARNIVAL.

His music has received airplay on Nigerian radio stations, while his video for Stacking appeared on Soundcity TV. In 2026, he released KALA featuring Caften, accompanied by an official music video.
